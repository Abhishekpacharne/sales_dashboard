# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based dashboard designed to analyze and visualize sales performance across different months, regions, product categories, and business segments.

It provides valuable business insights through KPI cards, charts, and interactive slicers to monitor sales performance and profitability.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze month-wise sales performance.
- Track total sales, total profit, and profit margin.
- Compare sales across different business segments.
- Analyze regional sales performance.
- Understand product category-wise sales distribution.
- Create an interactive dashboard for better decision-making.

- Total Sales

- Profit Margin

### 2. Segment-Wise Sales Analysis
Analyzes sales across:
- Consumer

- Home Office

### 3. Region-Wise Sales Analysis
Compares sales performance across:
- Central
- East
- South
- West

### 4. Category-Wise Sales Distribution
Visualizes sales distribution across:
- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

### 5. Monthly Sales Analysis
Analyzes month-wise sales trends and performance.

### 6. Interactive Filters
Uses Excel Slicers to filter dashboard data by month.
